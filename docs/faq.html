Home questions you probably have

Common questions,
our answers.

Do I need to know how to code?
  • No.
  • Campers who have never written a line of code build a working app in their first session.
  • Already know how to code? You'll get even more out of it — you'll catch things other people miss.
Isn't this just letting a robot do my homework?
  • Fair question. We say this in class too.
  • The AI writes the code.
  • You decide what gets built, catch what's wrong, and steer the whole thing. That's most of what real engineers do all day.
  • We also tell you plainly what you haven't learned yet, and exactly how to go learn it.
What do I need to bring?
  • Nothing.
  • Lab computers are set up and ready.
  • No installs. No accounts. Nothing to download.
  • Just show up.
How many sessions is it?
  • Middle school: one session, 85 minutes.
    • Build an app, break it, fix it, and take it home. All in that one session.
  • High school: four sessions — 120, 180, 180, and 60 minutes.
    • The last one is all demos.
What if I miss a session?
  • Session 1 is built to stand on its own. Can only make one session? Make that one.
  • High school: every later session starts with a quick catch-up for anyone new.
What language do we use?
  • Python, plus a toolkit called tkinter that makes windows and buttons.
  • Why tkinter: nothing to install, and a real window on screen in about sixty seconds.
  • You can build a web page instead, with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
  • Already know a different language? Talk to your teacher.
  • Really, you can use any language you want. The AI can write in most of them — the idea is what matters, not the language.
Do I get to keep what I build?
  • Yes. It's yours.
  • Before you leave, copy it into a Google Drive folder with your name on it. It'll be waiting for you at home.
  • Middle school campers, use this Google Drive folder.
  • High school campers, use this Google Drive folder.
  • You can also save it to a flash drive, or email it to yourself.
Can I do vibe coding at home?
  • Yes.
  • Claude Code, the tool you used in class, costs money — it needs a paid account.
  • Other tools that also cost money:
    • A Claude Pro subscription
    • A ChatGPT Pro subscription
    • A Gemini Pro subscription
    • A Mistral Education subscription
  • Recommended, and free: OpenCode, using its free models. No account needed to start. A paid Zen Go subscription is there too, if you want more than the free models give you.
  • See the OpenCode Zen how-to for how to set it up.
Can I do vibe coding without using AI in the cloud (data center)?
  • Yes. This is called a local model — an AI that runs right on your own computer, with no internet needed.
  • The catch: it needs powerful hardware, like a big graphics card (GPU), to run well.
  • See the local models how-to for hardware needs and setup steps.
