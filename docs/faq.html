Fair questions,
real answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Really, truly no. Not "no, but it helps if you do." Campers who have never written a line of code build a working app in their first session. If you do already code, you'll get even more out of it, because you'll catch things other people miss.

Isn't this just letting a robot do my homework?

Fair question, and we're upfront about it in class. The AI writes the code. You decide what gets built, catch what's wrong, and steer the whole thing. That's most of what real engineers spend their day doing. We also tell you plainly what you haven't learned yet, and exactly how to go learn it.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing. Lab laptops are set up and ready. No installs, no accounts, nothing to download. Just show up.

What if I miss a session?

Session 1 is built to work all by itself. You can come to just that one and still leave with a finished app. Every later session starts with a quick catch-up for anyone new.

What language do we use?

Python, plus a toolkit called tkinter that makes windows and buttons. We picked it because there's nothing to install, and you get a real window on screen in about sixty seconds. Already know a different language? Talk to your teacher.

Do I get to keep what I build?

Yes. It's yours. At the end of every session you copy it into a Google Drive folder with your name on it, so it's waiting for you at home.

Looking for your session notes?

Middle School Four sessions, 85 minutes each. Notes for every session. High School Four sessions, 120 / 180 / 180 / 60 minutes. Notes for every session.
